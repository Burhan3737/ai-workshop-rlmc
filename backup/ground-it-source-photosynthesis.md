## Slide 1 — What photosynthesis is
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae, and some bacteria convert **light energy into chemical energy** stored in glucose. It uses carbon dioxide and water, and releases oxygen as a by-product.

**Overall word equation:** carbon dioxide + water → (light energy, chlorophyll) → glucose + oxygen

**Balanced equation:** 6 CO₂ + 6 H₂O + light energy → C₆H₁₂O₆ + 6 O₂

The energy is captured by the green pigment **chlorophyll**.

---

## Slide 2 — Where it happens
Photosynthesis takes place in the **chloroplast**, an organelle found mainly in leaf mesophyll cells. Two regions matter:

- **Thylakoid membranes** (stacked into grana): hold chlorophyll and the machinery of the **light-dependent reactions**.
- **Stroma** (the fluid around the thylakoids): where the **light-independent reactions (Calvin cycle)** occur.

---

## Slide 3 — The two stages, at a glance
1. **Light-dependent reactions** — happen in the thylakoid membrane; need light; split water; produce **ATP**, **NADPH**, and **oxygen**.
2. **Light-independent reactions / Calvin cycle** — happen in the stroma; do not use light directly; use the ATP and NADPH from stage 1 to build **glucose** from CO₂.

Stage 2 depends on the products of stage 1, so in practice both run while the plant is in the light.

---

## Slide 4 — Light-dependent reactions
- Chlorophyll in **Photosystem II** absorbs light, exciting electrons to a higher energy level.
- **Photolysis of water:** light energy splits water (H₂O) into **oxygen, hydrogen ions (H⁺), and electrons.** The oxygen released by a plant comes from **water**, not from CO₂.
- Excited electrons pass down an **electron transport chain**, and the energy released is used to make **ATP**.
- **Photosystem I** re-energises the electrons, which are used to reduce **NADP⁺ to NADPH.**

**Products carried to the next stage:** ATP (energy) and NADPH (reducing power). **Oxygen** is a by-product and diffuses out of the leaf.

---

## Slide 5 — The Calvin cycle (light-independent reactions)
Occurs in the **stroma**. It "fixes" carbon dioxide into sugar using the ATP and NADPH made in the light reactions.

1. **Carbon fixation:** CO₂ is attached to a 5-carbon molecule, **RuBP**, by the enzyme **RuBisCO**.
2. **Reduction:** the resulting molecules are converted, using **ATP** and **NADPH**, into **G3P (glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate)**, a 3-carbon sugar.
3. **Regeneration:** most G3P is used (with more ATP) to regenerate RuBP so the cycle can continue; some G3P is used to build **glucose** and other organic molecules.

It is called "light-independent" because light is not used *directly* — but it stops soon after the light reactions stop, because it runs out of ATP and NADPH.

---

## Slide 6 — Factors that affect the rate
Three main **limiting factors** — the rate is capped by whichever is in shortest supply:

- **Light intensity** — more light → faster rate, up to a point (then another factor limits).
- **Carbon dioxide concentration** — usually the main limiting factor in normal conditions.
- **Temperature** — enzymes (e.g. RuBisCO) work faster as it warms, but too hot **denatures** them and the rate falls.

---

## Slide 7 — Why it matters
- Produces the **glucose** that feeds almost all food chains (plants are producers).
- Releases the **oxygen** most living things need to respire.
- Removes **carbon dioxide** from the atmosphere, linking it to the carbon cycle and climate.

---

## Key terms
- **Chlorophyll** — green pigment that absorbs light energy.
- **Chloroplast** — organelle where photosynthesis happens.
- **Thylakoid / stroma** — sites of the light-dependent / light-independent reactions.
- **Photolysis** — light-driven splitting of water.
- **ATP / NADPH** — energy and reducing power made in the light reactions.
- **RuBisCO / RuBP** — the enzyme and the 5-carbon molecule that fix CO₂.
- **G3P** — the 3-carbon sugar product of the Calvin cycle.
- **Limiting factor** — the factor in shortest supply that caps the rate.

## Common misconceptions (good for a "flip it" quiz)
- ❌ *"The oxygen comes from carbon dioxide."* ✅ It comes from the **splitting of water**.
- ❌ *"The light-independent reactions happen at night / in the dark."* ✅ They usually run in the **light**, because they depend on the ATP and NADPH the light reactions supply. "Light-independent" only means light isn't used *directly*.
- ❌ *"Plants only photosynthesise and don't respire."* ✅ Plants **respire all the time** as well.
- ❌ *"Glucose is made in the light-dependent stage."* ✅ Glucose is built in the **Calvin cycle**.
